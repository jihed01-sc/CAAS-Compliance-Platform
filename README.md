## 🚨 Important Notice: Unified Enterprise Compliance Framework

We are proud to announce the development and integration of a Unified Enterprise Compliance Framework within the CAAS platform. This advanced framework consolidates and harmonizes the strengths of three major compliance standards—ISO 27001, IEC, and NIS2—delivering a single, robust solution for comprehensive organizational compliance.

**Key Advantages of the Unified Framework:**
- Seamless cross-framework control mapping and equivalency
- Centralized risk and assessment methodology
- Streamlined evidence and progress tracking across all standards
- Enhanced reporting and analytics for enterprise-wide compliance posture

This strategic enhancement positions the CAAS platform as a leader in compliance management, empowering organizations to efficiently meet global regulatory requirements with confidence.

> For full details on the Unified Framework structure and usage, refer to the `Unified_Framework.xlsx` and supporting documentation in the `docs/` directory.
