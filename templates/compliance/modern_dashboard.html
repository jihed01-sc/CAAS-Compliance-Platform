ISO 27001

Information Security Management Systems

ISO/IEC 27001 is the world's best-known standard for information security management systems (ISMS). It defines requirements an ISMS must meet. The ISO 27001 standard provides companies of any size and from all sectors of activity with guidance for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an information security management system.

Compliance with ISO 27001 means that an organization or business has put in place a system to manage risks related to the security of data owned or handled by the company, and that this system respects all the best practices and principles enshrined in this International Standard.

The standard includes people, processes and technology. This systematic approach provides confidence to customers and other stakeholders that information security risks are properly managed.

Key Features

  • Risk Management Framework
  • 114 Security Controls
  • Continuous Improvement
  • International Recognition
  • Stakeholder Confidence
  • Legal Compliance
NIS

NIS 2.0

Network and Information Systems Directive

The NIS2 Directive is the EU's updated legislation on cybersecurity that aims to strengthen the security of network and information systems across the European Union. It replaces the original NIS Directive and introduces stricter cybersecurity requirements for a broader range of sectors.

NIS2 expands the scope of regulated entities, introduces more stringent security measures, and establishes harmonized sanctions across EU member states. Organizations covered by NIS2 must implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to manage cybersecurity risks.

The directive emphasizes the importance of incident reporting, supply chain security, and cross-border cooperation in cybersecurity matters within the EU.

Key Features

  • Network Security
  • Incident Reporting
  • Supply Chain Security
  • EU-wide Harmonization
  • Significant Penalties
  • Cross-border Cooperation
IEC

IEC 62443

Industrial Communication Networks Cybersecurity

IEC 62443 is a series of standards that addresses cybersecurity for operational technology (OT) in automation and control systems. It provides a flexible framework to address and mitigate current and future security vulnerabilities in industrial automation and control systems (IACS).

The standard takes a defense-in-depth approach with multiple layers of security measures. It covers the entire lifecycle of industrial automation systems, from initial risk assessment through design, implementation, maintenance, and eventual decommissioning.

IEC 62443 is particularly important for critical infrastructure sectors such as energy, water, manufacturing, and transportation where cybersecurity incidents could have significant physical and economic consequences.

Key Features

  • Industrial Systems Focus
  • Defense-in-Depth
  • OT Security
  • Security Levels
  • Lifecycle Management
  • Critical Infrastructure
IAM

IAM

Identity and Access Management

Identity and Access Management (IAM) is a framework of policies and technologies for ensuring that the right people in an enterprise have the appropriate access to technology resources. IAM systems fall under the overarching umbrellas of IT security and data management.

IAM includes the management of individual identities, their authentication, authorization, roles, and privileges within or across system and enterprise boundaries with the goal of increasing security and productivity while decreasing cost, downtime, and repetitive tasks.

Modern IAM implementations often include features like single sign-on (SSO), multi-factor authentication (MFA), privileged access management (PAM), and user lifecycle management to provide comprehensive identity governance.

Key Features

  • Identity Verification
  • Access Control
  • Single Sign-On (SSO)
  • Multi-Factor Authentication
  • User Lifecycle Management
  • Privileged Access Management
