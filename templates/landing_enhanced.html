Supported Security Frameworks

Our CAAS platform specializes in these internationally recognized cybersecurity frameworks, providing comprehensive compliance management and assessment capabilities.

NIS2

NIS 2 Directive

Network and Information Systems Directive 2.0 - Enhanced cybersecurity requirements for critical entities across the EU. Comprehensive risk management, incident response, and supply chain security measures.

Risk Management Incident Response Supply Chain Security Critical Infrastructure
ISO

ISO 27001/27002

International standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS). Provides a systematic approach to managing sensitive company information and ensuring comprehensive security controls implementation.

ISMS Management Security Controls Continuous Improvement Certification Ready
NIST

NIST Cybersecurity Framework

National Institute of Standards and Technology framework providing a policy framework of computer security guidance. Includes Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover functions for comprehensive cyber risk management.

Risk Assessment Asset Management Threat Detection Recovery Planning
IEC

IEC 62443

Industrial Communication Networks - IT Security for automation and control systems. Specialized framework for industrial cybersecurity covering OT environments, SCADA systems, and critical infrastructure protection.

Industrial Security OT Protection SCADA Security Zone & Conduit
ANSSI

ANSSI Guidelines

French National Cybersecurity Agency guidelines and recommendations. Comprehensive cybersecurity measures for French organizations, including critical infrastructure protection and government sector compliance.

French Compliance Critical Systems Government Standards National Security
CSA

CSA Cloud Security

Cloud Security Alliance framework for cloud computing security. Provides comprehensive guidelines for cloud service providers and consumers, including data protection, privacy, and compliance in cloud environments.

Cloud Security Data Protection Privacy Controls Multi-Cloud
