About Fortress Plus

Fortress Plus is an IT/OT cybersecurity and physical security consulting firm, founded in January 2021 and based in France with operations in Tunisia.

We bring together top experts and experienced consultants to offer a wide range of security services (360°) tailored to the needs of our clients across multiple sectors.

All our offers are based on the best practices of international security standards and norms (NIST, ISO 27x, IEC 62443, CSA, ASIS, NIS 2, LPM, ANSSI, etc.) and on our extensive multi-sector experience.

The Power of Integration

At Fortress Plus Enterprise, we've revolutionized cybersecurity compliance by creating a unified framework that seamlessly integrates the three most critical international standards: NIS2 Directive, ISO 27001/27002, and IEC 62443. This innovative approach eliminates the complexity and redundancy of managing multiple separate frameworks, providing organizations with a comprehensive, streamlined, and highly effective cybersecurity solution.

Our unified framework delivers 360° security coverage, addressing regulatory compliance (NIS2), information security management (ISO 27001/27002), and industrial control systems security (IEC 62443) through a single, cohesive methodology. This integration enables organizations to achieve multiple certifications simultaneously while reducing implementation costs, minimizing audit overhead, and maximizing security effectiveness across all operational domains.
